# Fresh-player test packet

Run three sessions with people who have not seen the story or source code. The builder's end-to-end test is not a fresh-player session.

## Give each player

“This is a first-person mystery in a museum after closing. Search with the flashlight, approach objects and inspect them with E, collect records, and work out what happened. There is no timer and wrong theories do not remove progress.”

Use a fresh browser profile or the explicit new-investigation control. For real-model sessions, finish its initial download before starting the playtime clock, and record download time separately. Let players discover the controls without coaching; intervene only if they are unable to proceed, and record the intervention.

After the ending, ask: “Who was missing from the museum's history?” Then: “Which discovery changed how you understood the room?” Finally: “Was anything confusing or frustrating?” Do not suggest the desired emotional response.

## Release decisions

- The 25–35 minute target is confirmed only when observed in fresh sessions.
- All three players should reach the ending without a developer giving puzzle solutions.
- Revise any record pairing that repeatedly feels arbitrary.
- Assess the first model download separately from the fictional experience.
- Keep unsupported questions and model failures in the report, not just successful examples.
- Obtain permission before using someone's name, recording or quotation in a public article. No outreach or recording is implied by this packet.
